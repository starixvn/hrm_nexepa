Stars Assessment Desciption
0 star N/A Unable to judge, if the nature or extent of your interaction does not enable you to provide feedback on a particular category for a particular individual, select the “NA” rating.
1 to 2 stars Poor Needs much Improvement: refers to performance which fails to measure up to standard and must be improved through corrective action steps.
3 to 4 stars Below Average Partially meets expectations: refers to performance and skills which are adequate in some respects but not fully up to standard; some corrective action is needed.
5 to 6 stars Satisfactory Meets expectations: refers to solid performance with a full skills base; most people fall into this category.
7 to 8 stars Good Exceeds expectations: refers to significant performance ability; only very strong performers should receive this rating.
9 to 10 stars Excellent Substantially exceeds expectations: refers to truly outstanding performance; should be someone you regard as a role model in the area rated.
